Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective on the public launch date · Draft for owner and counsel review before publishing.

PaperTendies ("we", "us") makes a fantasy trading game. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. PaperTendies is a simulation: no real money, bank accounts, or securities are ever involved. We don't sell your personal information and we don't show third-party ads.

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, a hashed password, the date you accepted the Terms, and an optional invite code you entered.
  • Gameplay: your virtual portfolio, orders and trades, season results, XP history, missions, streaks, achievements, cosmetics, battles, Market Call picks, raid damage, crew, and Season Pass progress.
  • Content you post: feed posts, comments, votes, reports you file, users you block, and feedback you send (with the app version and screen).
  • Notifications: if you allow push notifications, a device push token, your platform, your time zone (for quiet hours), and your notification preferences.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance traces (via Sentry): device model, OS version, app version, and the error. We don't attach your email.
  • Product analytics (via PostHog, if enabled): events like "completed Boot Camp" or "opened a supply drop", tied to a random account ID. Never your email, username, posts, or portfolio contents. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to the Arena Pass, Apple/Google processes payment. Our subscription provider (RevenueCat) tells us whether your subscription is active, linked to your account ID. We never see your card details.

What we don't collect

  • No real financial accounts, bank details, brokerage connections, or government IDs.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the game: portfolios, seasons, leaderboards, battles, missions, rewards, and the feed.
  • To keep it fair and safe: anti-cheat, content filtering, and handling reports and blocks.
  • To send notifications you've allowed (at most 2 reminders a day, never between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. in your time zone).
  • To fix bugs and improve the game.

What other players see

Your username, character, title, crew, level, season rank, portfolio value and return, battle results, and anything you post are visible to other players. NPCs and LIVE_WIRE episodes may mention your username when you climb the standings; turn this off in Settings → Privacy.

AI-generated content

Some NPC posts and story episodes are written by AI models (Anthropic or Cerebras). We send them game context such as public standings and usernames of players who haven't opted out. We don't send emails or private data.

Service providers

We use providers to host and run the Service: cloud hosting (database and cache), email delivery for password resets, Expo (push delivery), Sentry (crash reports), PostHog (analytics), RevenueCat (subscription status), and market-data providers (no personal data is sent to them). They process data only on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete your account any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account). We immediately delete your account and all associated gameplay data, posts, comments, votes, push tokens, and sessions. Backups roll off within 30 days.
  • Login sessions expire after 60 days of inactivity.
  • Crash and analytics data is kept for up to 1 year.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data, or object to processing. Email privacy@papertendies.app and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

PaperTendies isn't directed to children under 13 and requires users to confirm they are 13 or older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted in transit, and access tokens are short-lived. No system is perfectly secure; please use a unique password.

Changes and contact

We'll post changes here and notify you in the app for material changes. Questions: privacy@papertendies.app.
