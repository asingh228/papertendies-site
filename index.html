    The app

    A real trading app.
    Fake money.

    PaperTendies is a paper-trading app: you trade real stocks at real prices, but the money is pretend. Learn how the market works, test a strategy, or just see if you're any good, without risking a cent.

    • $100,000 to startPaper money, fresh every season.
    • Real stocks, live pricesSearch any ticker and trade at the live quote.
    • Every order typeMarket, limit, stop, stop-limit, trailing stop and short selling.
    • Dollars or sharesFractional shares, so $50 buys a slice of anything.
    • Your whole portfolioPositions, profit and loss, buying power and every trade you've made.
    • No real money, everNothing to deposit and nothing to withdraw. It's practice that feels real.

    This isn't a trading app with a leaderboard bolted on. It's a boss fight on the real stock market, and the boss cheats.

    TRANSMISSION

    The boss

    VEKTOR

    Null Capital's trading machine. He starts every season with $250,000 and one job: finish above you. The fight is measured in % return, so your $100,000 can beat his quarter million.

    VEKTOR THREAT STABLE
    • Price FuzzPrices flash fake digits. Real prices never change.
    • Skittish ButtonsConfirm buttons flinch away from your finger.
    • Packet LossEvery screen change stalls behind a lag screen.
    • Chart CorruptionFake lines scribbled over your charts.
    • LockdownEvery order needs a manual unlock before it sends.

    He attacks in surges, then burns out and recharges, and while he recharges every effect lifts. You can't skip his handicaps. You trade through them.

    The 101st floor

    Vektor takes orders.
    The Ceiling gives them.

    Five people own the platform. Every month they hand Vektor a new Directive, and a new story plays out in the city below, one episode a day. Their one law: the Gap never closes.
