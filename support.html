Frequently asked

Is this real money?

No. PaperTendies is 100% a game. You trade with virtual "tendies." No real securities are bought or sold and nothing here is investment advice.

How do seasons work?

Each calendar month is a season. Everyone starts with the same $100,000 in virtual cash, trades real tickers at real prices during market hours, and races up the Podium and past Vektor's line. Standings lock at the last close of the month, rewards go out, and everyone resets.

What is the Arena Pass?

An optional monthly subscription that unlocks the premium Season Pass lane (cosmetics) plus small conveniences like an extra mission reroll. It never changes your cash, fills, or rank. Everyone competes on equal terms.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ••• on any post to report it or block the author. Manage blocked users in Settings. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PaperTendies. On Android: Play Store → Subscriptions → PaperTendies.
